The Fantasy Trope Stack draws one trope for each of four roles: the Protagonist, the Conflict, the Setting, and the Theme. Pick a Subgenre to draw only from tropes that fit it, or leave it on Any for a cross-genre mix. Reroll a slot that fights the others, and lock the ones you want to keep. Copy carries the stack with a one-line gloss on each trope.
