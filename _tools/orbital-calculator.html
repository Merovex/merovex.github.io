Parent body

The star (or planet, for a moon) this world orbits. It sets the Hill sphere: how far out the world's gravity can hold a satellite against its parent's pull.

The formula

h = ∛( G · M · T² ⁄ 4π² ) − R
h
Altitude of the satellite above the surface
G
Gravitational constant, 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹
M
Mass of the planet
T
Rotation period of the planet
R
Radius of the planet
