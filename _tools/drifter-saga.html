Season 1 of the saga is a Drifter season — of the four archetypes that sustain an episodic A/B/C run, the Drifter is the one whose spine lives inside the man. The season Question is whether the road is all there is for him or whether he will find a reason worth riding it for: a Stance question, not an institution question. So the C-Spine, the Code, and the Stance are locked — rolled once and never touched — and each of the six books rolls only its own local color against them.

Re-roll the place and the foe freely; each book's run, destination, obstacle, and antagonist-of-the-week is local. Do not pin the Edge or the System — a Drifter who keeps returning to the same place and the same foe quietly becomes a Lawman or a Homesteader, and the form goes soft. That's why the spine panel below is the only thing held.

Season 1 — The C-Spine (Drifter)

Question
Is the road all there is for a man like him, or will he find a reason worth riding it for?
Stake
The bird is the last of his mentor and the only home he has, and a ship has to fly to stay a ship: dock fees, fuel, a living, earned one run at a time. But hulls and lanes are both too scarce to leave idle, so both fall under the old escheat law: a corridor untraveled, a hull unflown, escheats to the state, and the Scout Service is the state. Sit still to grieve and the Service takes the last thing the old man left him. Keep moving and the only runs that pay are the runs that feed the machine, while every dead lane he reopens maps where he's been, for the Service to claim and the gang to follow.
Terminus
The last run ends with the one it was meant for already lost to the dark, but somewhere along it he stopped riding to keep the ship and started riding to keep the promise, and he cannot unknow it. He rides on, no longer adrift, carrying for the first time the dangerous thought that the runs are too important to leave to desperate men, and that someone ought to build a thing that outlasts them.
Key
The aimless road become a calling, the calling not yet a home.
Code
A delivery is a bond worth dying for. The C-story exists to test it, so every re-rolled run is a fresh trial of the same value.
Stance
Adrift. The road as identity and as escape; the man who keeps moving so that nothing can hold him, and tells himself that is freedom. The Terminus is this stance cracking.

Stoddard Locke is the standing antagonist for the whole saga, but in Season 1 he is weather, not the per-book villain — the ambient pressure the season rides under, felt at the edges and named in the C-clicks. The foe each book turns on is rolled fresh and left behind. The moment Locke becomes the recurring nemesis squared off with book after book, the season has changed archetype.
