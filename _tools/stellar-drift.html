--- layout: tool order: 60 title: Stellar Drift — Conlang Engine description: "Seed a language onto a world, isolate it for a thousand years, and read back the daughter tongue. Deterministic: the same seed and world card always rebuild the identical language." ---

Take roughly today's top languages, seed them across 150 worlds around 2700 AD, and isolate each world for a thousand years — the Beowulf-to-now gap. This engine models that drift for a single world. Every output is mutually unintelligible with its parent, and one parent seeded onto several worlds yields a family as diverse as Romance.

A seed plus a world card is the save file. Identical inputs always rebuild the identical language — sentences, glossary, and log byte for byte.
