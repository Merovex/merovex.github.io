The SF & Fantasy Pen Name Generator rolls eight names at a time. Pick a Style for the shelf you're writing for, and a Form: a female or male first name, or two initials, which SF and fantasy authors have long used and which keep the author's gender off the cover. Click any name to copy it. Surnames of well-known SF and fantasy authors are never used, but search any name you like before you put it on a book.
