The C-Story Engine is the companion to the Western Engine plot generator. The Western Engine rolls the A-story — the self-contained episode that opens and closes inside one installment. This rolls the C-story: the serialized continuity spine that advances one click per episode and pays off across the whole arc.

A C-story is generated once per series, not per episode. You roll a spine — a Question, a Stake, a Terminus, and a signature Key — then the generator distributes its clicks across the episodes on a plant / nudge / tag rhythm, so each installment advances the arc exactly one notch while its A-story resolves clean.

Honest-broker scope: only three archetypes sustain an episodic A/B/C run, so only Lawman, Drifter, and Trek carry a portable spine. The other four terminate or resist episodic structure; select one and the engine returns a note explaining why, instead of a spine. That's deliberate.

Season Shaper

The C-Story Engine rolls what the serialized spine is. The Season Shaper lays out where it goes. Give it a per-episode scene count and a season length, and it shapes the A/B/C structure two ways at once: within each episode it splits the scenes into A (the self-contained plot, which always opens and closes), B (relational / thematic counterpoint woven through the gaps), and C (the serialized spine click); across the season it assigns each episode a C-story role — plant, complicate, turn, deepen, pressure, terminus — so the spine resolves on the finale and never sags in the middle. Eight-episode seasons get a double-pump (a false terminus, then a reset) to keep the stretch from going soft.
