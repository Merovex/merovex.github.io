# Gendaldea — Decline-Era Adjustment Ledger

Back-projects the (corrected) Imperium baseline to the **First Decline** — the percolation trough between the Expanse peak and the Imperium recovery. Astrophysics invariant; trade/travel codes & T5 extensions recomputed at apply-time (delta method).

**Floors (setting-wide, advanced-society minimum):** Expanse TL 7 · **Decline TL 6** · **Imperium TL 7** (baseline raised: 50 non-canon worlds → TL 7; 4 canon preserved below it & flagged).

**Rules applied**

- **Population** = baseline−1 (floor 1) — still GROWING through the Decline. Scars (Imperium pop 0): Expanse 4 → **Decline 2** → Imperium 0. Rosendanio (1018) flagged.
- **Connectivity** = jump range collapses 2→1; J-1 clusters decide who stays linked (56 isolated singletons).
- **Tech** = baseline − regression (J1 size: singleton −4 / 2–4 −3 / 5–7 −2 / 8+ −1; −1 if pop≥7, −1 if Ri/In), **floored at TL 6** and never above the Imperium value. No stone-age crashes — worst worlds bottom at early-stellar TL 6.
- **Governance** = founding-basis blocs fragment: coordinated polities (1,2,3,4,8,9) → balkanised (7); feudal/colony (5,6) survive; isolated singletons → warlord (10) / anarchy (0). Canon kept.
- **Starport** = decay by isolation (singleton −2, small cluster −1). **Bases** = N/S stripped.

**Totals:** 229 worlds adjusted · worlds below TL6 (canon exceptions only): 1 · isolated singletons: 56.
