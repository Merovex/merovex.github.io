# Gendaldea — Islands of the First Expanse

*Gendaldea and the **Expanse** are an original setting (coined 2006); no relation to any similarly-named franchise.*

Each island is a jump-2 cluster — the political unit of the archipelago. A single **founding basis** per island fixes its anchor (settlement origin), its **governance center**, and its economy, and threads its arc across the three eras (First Expanse → First Decline → Imperium). Procedural worlds sit within ±2 government levels of the center; canon worlds keep authored codes. Stats are **First Expanse** values unless noted. Working names are placeholders.


## The Corvallisio Estates

**Anchor (founding world):** Corvallisio (0823) · **Systems:** 51 (21 canon) · **Subsectors:** E×3, F×5, I×7, J×19, K×6, M×6, N×5 · **Affiliation (by Imperium):** Imperium — Annexed

**Founding basis.** Founded by landed **Oligarch clans** who claimed Corvallisio's garden belt as agrarian estates, then spread outward to work the surrounding farm and garden worlds. Governance settled into a **feudal technocracy** of estate-holders. Its agricultural wealth made it the prize of the consolidation wars; by the Imperium it is the recently-**annexed breadbasket**, still smarting under occupation.
