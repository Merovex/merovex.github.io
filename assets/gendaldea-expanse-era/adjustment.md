# Gendaldea — Expanse-Era Adjustment Ledger

Back-projects the Imperium-height baseline to the **end of the First Expanse** (greatest extent, pre-Decline). Astrophysics is invariant; only the human overlay changes. Trade/travel codes are derived downstream and not listed here.

**Rules applied**

- **Population** = baseline−2 (floor 1); the 6 pop-0 "scar" worlds are settled (alive in the Expanse, they die in the Decline).
- **Tech** = peak **13**, drop 1 TL per **2 hops** from each island’s anchor (highest-pop world). Floor TL 7.
- **Dependency** = **TL ≤ 8** worlds have no jump drive; they depend on hub worlds and Imudring traders for inter-system travel.
- **Governance** (procedural only) = pulled within ±2 of an island political center (canon-set for Core/Annexed, anchor-set elsewhere); imperial captive-government (code 6) stripped → Oligarchic (3).
- **Starports** = X-ports raised to E (no portless worlds pre-Decline).
- **Bases** = imperial Naval/Scout bases stripped (no Imperium yet).
- **Canon** (gold-shaded) worlds: demographics/tech back-projected like all worlds; governance & authored lore left untouched.

**Totals:** 229 of 229 worlds adjusted · 45 canon worlds (conservative) · 15 dependent worlds (TL≤8).

## Dependent worlds (TL ≤ 8 — no jump drive)

These are the 15 procedural frontier worlds that cannot build their own jump ships at the end of the Expanse. They are settled, populated, and growing, but rely on hub worlds and Imudring traders for every inter-system link — the natural first casualties when the Decline severs the network, and ready-made story settings for an Expanse-era series.
